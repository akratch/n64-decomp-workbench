# CV64 worked-example notice

The original private campaign inputs were generated from a legally obtained
Castlevania 64 US 1.0 ROM and from
[`k64ret/cv64`](https://github.com/k64ret/cv64) at commit
`5307217aa772019b7576cad3cb2c545e88e0394a`. Each local bundle contained
target assembly derived from the game binary, generated project context, and
a candidate source file.

That provenance is attribution; it does not grant a license or place those
third-party materials under this repository's CC0 dedication. No affirmative
redistribution basis was established, so the payloads are not present in this
repository or its distributions.

If a future change proposes restoring those payloads, the maintainer must
first either:

1. record the permission or other redistribution basis that covers every
   bundled input; or
2. keep them excluded and retain only a generation recipe and aggregate,
   non-code measurements.

This notice records how the release gate was resolved; it is not a legal
conclusion about reverse engineering or any contributor's private use.
