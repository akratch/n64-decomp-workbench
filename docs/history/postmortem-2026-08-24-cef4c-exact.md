# Hotwash: the cef4c exact-match endgame (2026-08-23/24)

The last unmatched function in `ssb-decomp-re` (`func_ovl0_800CEF4C`,
`lbParticleUpdateStruct`, 1,868 instructions) went from a 99.91% hosted
frontier through a fully reverse-engineered allocator, a one-word
compiler-phase wall, and out the other side to a **words=0 / exact=true**
source. This document is the after-action review of how the workbench
performed, what had to be built ad hoc, and the campaign plan for elevating
every lesson into product, knowledge, and skill.

## What the campaign actually took

- ~8,000 compiled-and-scored source variants across two agents' sessions
  (donor grids, schedule equilibria, layout families), most generated by
  asserted-anchor string rewriting and scored in parallel waves.
- A deep-instrumented IDO 7.1 `uopt` (five trace points beyond the shipped
  A71 pair: interference propagation, live-block bvects, transit bvects,
  exact `compute_save` priorities, neighbor lists) proving the complete
  Chow-priority model, then a phase-capture toolchain (arg-preserving
  wrappers around ugen/as0/as1) plus Ucode/Binasm decoding to attribute
  each residual to its owning pass.
- Ucode patch-and-replay to *prove* fixes at phase boundaries before
  hunting C for them (the conditional-fjp barrier was proven words=0 by
  stream surgery a full session before the source spelling landed).
- A final composition of four independently proven mechanisms — the
  triple-conditional branch-to-next barrier, goto-pair fallthrough
  inversion, Duff-nested dispatch layout, ternary selector temp — each
  useless alone, exact together.
- One last ELF/ROM autopsy showing the hosted target.o itself was
  extracted 20 bytes short of the function's true rodata (its literal
  pool), explaining a residual hosted score of 5 against a ROM-faithful
  candidate.

## What worked (keep and lean in)

1. **`compare --json` as the campaign backbone.** The metric family
   (words/raw/norm/regs/fp/opcodes/gaps/insns/frame/fdr) plus
   `diff_sites` classes drove every decision; the `exact`/`acceptance_basis`
   escalation surfaced correctly at the end. `window` for aligned rows was
   the right second step every time.
2. **The new Binasm/Ucode decoders** (`binasm.py`, `ucode.py`). Parsing
   both phase streams side-by-side found the two decisive facts of the
   endgame: the surviving conditional branch record before the `addu`, and
   the trampoline-collapse body relocation. These modules earned first-class
   status.
3. **Evidence-ladder discipline.** score → diff_sites → window →
   byte-pattern search → phase captures → instrumented trace → phase
   attribution. Every rung is cheaper than guessing at the next one up, and
   byte-pattern search (grep the discriminating instruction encodings
   across dozens of objects) is an underrated instant oracle.
4. **Fan-out with asserted anchors.** Cheap-model agents ran hundreds of
   mechanical variants safely because generators asserted every anchor
   string was unique before substitution; steering agents mid-flight with
   fresh discriminating evidence (the both-registers byte check) saved a
   full wasted sweep.

## What failed or was missing (the improvement backlog)

1. **`compare --symbol` is broken on multi-function objects** (garbage
   words/gaps; logged in `docs/known-defects.md`). The TU-state probe had
   to fall back to whole-.text alignment plus byte search.
2. **Installed-tool staleness.** The session's installed CLI predated
   `pass binasm`; everything ran via `PYTHONPATH` from the repo. The skill
   should teach the from-source invocation, and releases should be cut when
   new pass tooling lands mid-campaign.
3. **No phase-capture product.** The arg-preserving wrapper toolchain that
   captured ugen/as1 inputs/outputs was rebuilt ad hoc *twice* (two
   sessions, two agents). It is four shell wrappers and a directory
   convention — it should be `decomp-workbench capture`.
4. **No Ucode patch/replay product.** Stream surgery + stock-phase replay
   (byte-for-byte reproduction from a captured Ucode file, then patched
   variants) was written ad hoc twice as well, and it was the single
   highest-leverage technique of the endgame.
5. **Scalar metrics mislead on layout-shaped candidates.** `words`
   over-charged block permutations (1,791 "words" for a candidate whose
   true edit script was one moved 29-row block); the o3and family showed
   `opcodes` conflating schedule and allocation. Per-site heal signatures
   (a watchlist of discriminating rows rendered as healed/broken columns)
   were the fitness function that actually converged, and `align` should
   auto-run on structure-mismatch.
6. **The ad hoc sweep runner.** `score_wave.py` (compile pool at nice,
   score, signature table) was rewritten in three shapes; `campaign`
   exists but did not cover the compile-fan-out + signature workflow the
   endgame needed.
7. **Nobody audited the target.** Ten days of campaigns assumed target.o
   scope was truth; the final hosted residual was the *target's* missing
   literal pool, provable in one minute against ROM bytes. A target-audit
   step belongs at campaign start, not after words=0.
8. **Saturation claims were basin-local.** Two independently produced
   "exhausted" verdicts (donor rotations, empty-read positions) were
   falsified after equilibrium shifts. Negative results must record their
   basin/scope, and re-opening closed dials after any schedule change must
   be doctrine.

## New compiler knowledge earned (IDO 7.1, all evidence-backed)

- **as1 `peep_reg` copy propagation** and its `update_ctnt` fact-carry
  rule (fallthrough single-predecessor only; branch-target blocks start
  clean) — why one dispatch of a pair heals for free and the other never
  does.
- **UGEN's branch-to-next eliminator removes at most two conditional
  branches and unboundedly many unconditional ones.** Three chained
  empty-body conditional tests survive as exactly one removable branch —
  which as1 deletes *after* its transient block boundary kills the copy
  fact. Zero-cost, source-reachable as1 barrier. Four tests are
  catastrophic; two are erased.
- **uopt `if (c) goto L` normalization**: the goto target becomes the
  fallthrough (branch inverted onto the other path). Control flow at the
  target label stalls the transform; ballast statements on the opposing
  path re-bias it.
- **uopt relocates goto-labeled, table-referenced body blocks** next to
  their XJP (cfe preserves lexical order — attribution proven by phase
  capture). Case-anchoring adds predecessors and partially defeats it;
  Duff-nesting (inner switch entered by `goto` into the outer switch's
  body) reproduces the two-cluster dispatch layout exactly.
- **Ternary selector `x ? x : x` reshapes the selector temp** (and with an
  `&&`-chain condition, carries the barrier *inside* the switch statement),
  fixing stack temp-slot layout without codegen change.
- **The dead-read dial calculus** for globalcolor: priority = save/units
  with `units = raw<3 ? raw : ((raw-2)>>2)+2`, `raw = refs + cardbits`;
  +10 save per surviving ref regardless of branch nesting; +1 cardbit per
  spanning statement; ties break by ascending first-occurrence; empty-body
  `if` folds *after* `compute_save` (refs count, code doesn't); reads
  survive only on dead/undefined variables.
- **Literal pools are per-use-site** (four identical 2048/π literals, no
  dedup), extern loads are *not* equivalent to literals (aliasing class
  changes the schedule), and single-line macro expansion is byte-equivalent
  where multi-line is not (line-number effect).

## Campaign plan: elevating the lessons

Six waves, each shippable independently, ordered by evidence-weighted value.

### W1 — Knowledge: `docs/compiler-laws/ido-7.1.md` (highest value, no code)
Author the 7.1 law book from this campaign's evidence (the eight findings
above plus the allocator model), mirroring `ido-5.3.md`'s form, each law
with its probe artifact pointer. Wire the era into `guide`. Add the new
levers to the field guide: conditional-fjp barrier, goto-pair parity,
Duff-nesting, ternary selector temp, ballast bias, dead-read dial calculus.
Acceptance: `decomp-workbench guide ido-7.1` surfaces every law.

### W2 — Instrumentation productization
1. `capture`: emit the arg-preserving wrapper toolchain around any IDO
   root, collect per-phase before/after streams into labeled run dirs.
2. `pass replay-ugen` + `ucode patch`: replay a captured Ucode through
   stock ugen+as1 (byte-verified against the direct compile), apply
   record-level insertions/edits with label renumbering — the fjp-probe
   and part6 harnesses, unified.
3. Decoder hardening: Path/bytes overloads, classify the "unknown" Binasm
   records (tables, labels), `ucode window` / `binasm window` CLI, and a
   two-stream side-by-side diff command.
4. Ship the uopt-A71-v2 instrumentation recipe (interference, livebb,
   cardbv, save, neighbors) as a maintained patch + doc beside
   `compiler-instrumentation.md`.
Acceptance: the entire cef4c endgame is reproducible with product commands
and zero ad hoc scripts.

### W3 — Metrics and sweeps
1. Heal-signature scoring: `compare --watch-rows` (probe row set → per-site
   healed/broken columns) and the same in batch.
2. `sweep`: generator-driven compile fan-out (bounded jobs, nice), scored
   table with signature columns — the productized `score_wave.py`.
3. Layout-aware verdicts: on structure-mismatch, auto-run `align` and
   report the moved-block edit script beside `words`, so permutation
   candidates are never mis-ranked again.
4. Fix `compare --symbol` (known-defects.md) with a multi-function
   regression fixture from the TU probe.

### W4 — Target trust
`target audit`: verify a scratch/campaign target.o against ROM bytes and
section scope — rodata continuity after jump tables, literal-pool
presence/width, reloc-symbol externalization of function-owned data — and
say loudly when the target itself is wrong. The cef4c literal-pool case is
the fixture. This runs at campaign *registration*, not at the end.

### W5 — Skill and docs
Update the `n64-decomp-campaign` skill bundle: evidence-ladder v2 (phase
captures and byte-pattern oracles as rungs), the barrier/layout/dial
playbooks, prove-at-the-phase-boundary-then-hunt-C doctrine,
mechanism-composition doctrine (prove levers in isolation, compose late),
saturation-scope hygiene (negatives are basin-local; re-open dials after
equilibrium shifts), and the fan-out pattern (asserted-anchor generators,
cheap-model sweeps, mid-flight steering). Fold the cef4c narrative into
`final-function-campaigns.md`; changelog everything.

### W6 — Release
Version bump with W1-W5, quality checklist, GitHub release (PyPI still
gated to v1*). Add the cef4c streams and expected metrics as regression
fixtures for the decoders and replay.

## One-line summary

The workbench's comparison core carried the campaign; the wins came from
phase-boundary work it didn't yet productize — capture, patch, replay,
signature fitness, target audit — and every one of those is now specified
with its fixture in hand.
